“If you investment horizon is long—at least 25 or 30 years—there is only one sensible approach: Buy every month, automatically, and whenever else you can spare some money. Chp 9 also talks about choosing specific funds / letting others manage your funds and whether the returns are really all that much higher

Buffetology - Mary Buffet
I can’t remember if this one had any mention of investing in index but I think there was a bit of mention of index as well
